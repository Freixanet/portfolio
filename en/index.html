Alice.

Your own agent, on your iPhone.

iPhoneSwift and SwiftUI
WebReact and TypeScript
Server and agentsPython
StatusIn daily use

On a phone, an agent lives inside a messaging bot. That’s fine for questions. But when it buys something, signs in or needs your permission halfway through, you need something else: to see what it does, and to decide.

You ask it for something. Like you’d ask a person.

A native chat with live replies, attachments and a voice you can interrupt by speaking. One thing at a time.

You see what it does. While it does it.

The page the agent is on appears in the chat, live. Tap it to take over; when you’re done, hand it back.

It pays when you say “Pay”.

It fills in the basket, the address and the delivery, tries the discount codes and stops before paying. The card never goes through the chat.

Agents, notes, routines. One gesture away.

Morning and evening summaries, calendar, goals that measure themselves and reminders when you arrive somewhere, without sharing where you are.

I

The story

Why it exists, what I decided and what was hard.

The problem

Hermes is a very capable open agent, but on a phone you reach it through a messaging bot. That works for questions. It falls short when the agent buys something, signs in to a site or needs your permission halfway through a task.

The decisions

  • A native SwiftUI app, not a website inside an app.
  • No secrets in the chat: passwords, codes and cards go through secure sheets to your Mac.
  • An irreversible step, like paying, waits for an explicit “Pay”.
  • Official Hermes, unpatched: anything missing goes in a plugin of my own.

The hard part

Buying for real. Every shop is different and payment goes through the bank’s page. Alice tells a confirmed payment from a declined or unconfirmed one, and blocks a second payment at the same shop until it knows what happened to the first.

Where it is today

I use it every day for chat, calendar, goals and summaries. Purchases go as far as payment and have already surfaced real bugs that I’ve fixed. I wouldn’t yet let it buy unsupervised.
